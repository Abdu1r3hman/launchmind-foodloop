Frequently Asked Questions

How does the geolocation search work?

We use your device's location to show nearby discounted meals. You can also filter by distance manually.

Is the food safe to eat?

All listed meals are inspected by the restaurant and must be within their safe consumption window.

Can I list my own meals?

Yes! Restaurant owners can sign up and list surplus dishes through our dashboard.
