Key Features

📍 Geo‑filtered Listings

Shows only restaurants within a configurable radius (e.g., 2 km) of the user's location.

⏰ Time‑Based Dynamic Discounts

Deeper discounts appear as closing time nears, encouraging last‑minute purchases.

🛒 Instant Purchase & Reservation

Buy instantly with in‑app payment or reserve for pickup; QR code verification at the restaurant.

🔔 Push Notifications

Real‑time alerts for new deals from favorite restaurants or matching preferences.

⭐ Ratings & Reviews

Diners rate food and service, building trust and giving restaurants valuable feedback.
