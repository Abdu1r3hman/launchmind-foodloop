Affordable Meals. Zero Waste.

FoodLoop connects restaurants with surplus end‑of‑day meals to students and budget‑conscious diners, offering delicious food at steep discounts while cutting food waste.

Restaurants: Sign Up Diners: Browse Meals

How It Works

1

Restaurants List Surplus

At closing time, owners upload photos, portions, and pickup windows for unsold dishes.

2

Diners Browse & Buy

Nearby students and budget‑conscious users see real‑time listings, filter by price/distance, and purchase instantly.

3

Pick Up & Enjoy

Users collect their meal at the restaurant, savor a tasty deal, and both parties earn sustainability points.
