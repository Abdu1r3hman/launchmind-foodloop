The Problem

Every day, tons of perfectly good food go to waste while students and budget‑conscious diners struggle to afford meals. FoodLoop turns this loss into a win‑win: restaurants recover value, users save money, and the planet benefits.

How It Works

Restaurant Onboarding

Streamlined sign‑up flow to list daily surplus inventory, set prices, and define pickup windows.

Real‑Time Inventory

Live updates show current unsold items, quantities, and discount levels, refreshed every few minutes.

Geolocation Search

Map and list view filter meals by proximity, displaying distance and estimated travel time.

Secure Payments

In‑app checkout with multiple payment options, order confirmation, and real‑time status updates.

Reviews & Ratings

Post‑purchase feedback on food quality, service, and sustainability impact helps future buyers.

Eco Impact

Each meal saved translates to reduced CO₂ emissions and less landfill waste.

Nearby Listings

Interactive map showing discounted meals around you
